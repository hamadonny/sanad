**Description (up to 4000):**
Sanad is the jamiya (savings circle) you know — digital, organised and transparent.

Join a circle with the monthly amount you choose and see from day one what you pay, what you receive and when. Sanad gathers the group, collects every month and pays each month's recipient what the group paid. Sanad organises circles and does not guarantee them: members on early spots carry a holdback, and anyone who stops paying after receiving is banned and pursued legally.

Why Sanad?
• Organised for you: reminders, collection and payouts handled every month, and late money forwarded to you as soon as it arrives.
• Fully transparent: a clear calculator before you join and a schedule of every payment and payout.
• A fair draw you can verify yourself in the app.
• Want an early spot? Bid for it openly and see exactly what you will receive before you confirm.
• An electronic agreement signed with a one-time code, and a PDF receipt for every payment.
• Your privacy protected: other members never see your phone number or Civil ID, and your data is encrypted.
• Face ID / fingerprint lock to open the app and confirm every payment.
• Arabic and English, with Arabic numerals and Hijri dates if you like.

How it works

1. Verify your identity with your Civil ID.
2. Choose your monthly amount and pay the one-time organising fee (3% of the pot, refunded if the circle never starts).
3. When the circle is full, the draw sets your spot and schedule.
4. Pay each month and receive your pot on your date.

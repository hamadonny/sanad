Logo directions

Keystone archRecommended

The arch stands because of its keystone. The orange stone is Sanad, the organiser — the piece that holds the circle together. Strong at app-icon size, works in one colour.

The circle

Ten segments for ten members; the orange one is whoever receives this month, the centre is the pooled fund. Reads as "rotation" instantly.

Three pillars

The three teeth of «س» rising like savings that grow, sweeping into the letter’s tail. Most Arabic of the marks, but closer to a chart.

Wordmark

«سند» set in Readex Pro Bold, with the dot of «ن» in Flame — the organiser holding the group together. Used for the splash screen, contracts and receipts, next to any of the marks above.
